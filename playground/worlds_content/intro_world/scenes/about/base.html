Research purposes

Why we collect this data

What this is

A research playground for studying how digital marketing shapes attention and decision-making. Instead of explaining influence with lectures, we let visitors experience it directly and observe how awareness builds.

How your data helps

Behavioural data on persuasion in real interfaces is rarely available to independent researchers. Your anonymous interactions help us compare which approaches make people more aware of marketing techniques, and how digital literacy develops over time. The findings contribute to academic work on responsible marketing and digital well-being.

How we protect you

We never collect your name, email, or device identifiers. Only a pseudonymous ID kept in your browser links your clicks across scenes. You may withdraw at any time by clearing your browser data — past records are stored without anything tying them to you personally.
