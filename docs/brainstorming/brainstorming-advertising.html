Brainstorming the "Advertising" world narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

Outline how ads, sponsored content, and "native" placements appear inside the story—using fictional or composite brands to avoid defamation and trademark issues. Focus on recognition: targeting cues, retargeting metaphors, and disclosure patterns users can transfer to real feeds.

Ideas

  • Core problem (the thing we teach): Advertising often doesn’t just “say” something — it implies what you should want, who you should be, and what is “normal.” It can quietly create demand by embedding hidden assumptions. Example: an acne ad that shows someone hiding their skin and then becoming “confident” implies acne must be hidden.
  • Solution: Realazer (a truth-revealing tool): The player gets a device called the Realazer. They can “shoot” an ad (billboard / poster / in-feed placement) and it flips into an honest version — a funny “truthful billboard” that reveals the hidden premise behind the ad. The tone is playful, not dark: it’s satire that makes subtext visible.
  • Game progression (how users learn):
    1. Introduction: Gaia (or the guide) hands you the Realazer and says it reveals what ads imply, not only what they claim.
    2. Showcase: you see several ads, then shoot them to reveal their Realazed versions (the “honest rewrite”).
    3. Choice: for a given ad, you’re shown a few different Realazed rewrites and asked to pick your favorite. This teaches that there are multiple ways to articulate the hidden assumption (shame, scarcity, status, belonging).
    4. Create: you’re shown an unseen ad and asked to Realaze it yourself (free text, or guided building blocks like “hidden premise”, “emotion hook”, “unstated norm”).
  • Examples (recognizable patterns → Realazed “honest” versions):
    • Acne / “flaw” cover-up: “Cover it and feel confident.”
      Realazed: “We’re selling comfort by suggesting your skin should be hidden.”
    • Luxury / status: “Because you deserve it.”
      Realazed: “Buy this to feel higher-status — we’re linking worth to ownership.”
    • Perfume / identity: “Be irresistible.”
      Realazed: “We’re selling a fantasy: a product as a shortcut to love/attention.”
    • Urgency / scarcity: “Limited time only.”
      Realazed: “We’re borrowing your stress to make you decide faster.”
  • Win condition (the transfer): The goal is that once players leave the game, they start noticing the hidden premises in real ads automatically — seeing what’s implied, not just what’s said. The narrative reward is the Orb of Vision: you can now “see through” the subtext.
  • Bonus mechanic (real world → Realazer): The user can earn a small bonus “thingie” if they upload a real ad they encountered (e.g., a street poster or billboard) and propose their own Realazed version. This bridges the game to everyday life: “I saw it, I noticed the implication, I can rewrite it.”
