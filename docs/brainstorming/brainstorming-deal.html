Brainstorming the "Deal" world narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

Explore story beats, characters, and pacing for the Deal world: pricing psychology, discounts, anchoring, and how offers are framed. The goal is a coherent narrative where users encounter realistic deal mechanics, then debrief on what influenced them—aligned with the playground’s research and ethics guardrails.

Ideas

  • Open on a "market square" where every stall uses a different pricing trick; the user must complete a mission by recognising which technique is which.
  • Pair each deal pattern with an optional interventive message that names the pattern without breaking immersion.
  • Track hesitation time before accept/decline as a behavioural outcome for experiments comparing framing variants.
