Brainstorming the "Gamification" world narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

Draft how streaks, points, levels, and rewards drive engagement in-story—without using shame or fear as hooks. The world should showcase how game-like loops shape behaviour so users can recognise them in real apps and games.

Ideas

  • A guild or league plot where "daily login" and streak recovery are dramatised, then debriefed.
  • Optional competitive leaderboard as an experimental arm (with clear opt-in and intensity limits).
  • Collectible tokens that map to real marketing patterns (e.g. streak freeze as a monetisation parallel).
