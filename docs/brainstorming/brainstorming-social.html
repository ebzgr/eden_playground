Brainstorming the "Social" world narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

Develop the social proof and community-driven persuasion arc: testimonials, follower counts, "others bought" cues, and normative pressure—always within ethical intensity limits. The narrative should help users notice when bandwagons and FOMO are manufactured.

Ideas

  • A festival or forum setting where popularity meters and review walls drive choices; optional multi-user feel without real PvP at v1.
  • Characters who contradict fake consensus—teaching critical questions to ask when seeing social metrics.
  • End-beat self-report on belonging vs manipulation to feed well-being metrics.
