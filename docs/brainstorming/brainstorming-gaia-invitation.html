The invitation by Gaia narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

This is the overarching journey narrative for one of our mythic-themed runs. Users are reached through different channels (email, social, partners, events—whatever we enable) via the campaign "Invitation by Gaia: Be the knight of Eden". That invitation sets the tone: Gaia, as the divine personification of Earth, calls the user onto a hero's path.

After accepting, users move through the playground world by world, completing missions and gathering orbs (wisdom, vision, patience, and other tokens tied to each world). The arc culminates when they have collected everything needed: Gaia grants the Shield of Eden, and the user is recognised as the Knight of Eden by Gaia—our ambassador milestone for finishing the full narrative, with early access and visibility described elsewhere in the wiki (Worlds, Conceptual Layer).

This page is for brainstorming copy, beats, and channel-specific variants of the invitation and landing experience, while keeping intensity and ethics within our guardrails.

Ideas

  • Landing conversation with Gaia: short, voice-led or text-led script variants for A/B testing tone and clarity.
  • Per-channel teaser lines that all resolve to the same in-product invitation (trackable UTMs, same narrative spine).
  • Closing ceremony UI when the Shield is granted: debrief + consent-aware sharing of "Knight of Eden" status.
