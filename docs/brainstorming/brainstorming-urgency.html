Brainstorming the "Urgency" world narrative

Importance: Medium  ·  Status: Open

Shape the plot and setting for scarcity, countdowns, and "act now" pressure—at an intensity that mirrors everyday digital experiences without amplifying harm. Narrative should make urgency tactics visible and memorable while leaving room for debrief and self-report measures (stress, perceived pressure).

Ideas

  • A ticking clock tied to a fictional event (ticket release, festival gate) with ethical caps on copy intensity.
  • Branch where the user can "walk away" and see the same offer resurface later—compare regret vs relief.
  • Meta beat: a letter or message from the journey guide reflecting on urgency after the scene resolves.
