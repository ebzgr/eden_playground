Marketing for Betterment's playground

This research and awareness platform under the Marketing for Betterment initiative makes manipulative marketing techniques visible through immersive, art-driven experiences — and provides a scientific infrastructure for studying how those techniques evolve, who they affect, and how awareness can reduce their effectiveness.
